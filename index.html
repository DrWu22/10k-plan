Follow these 4 steps once to wire up your training log. Every session you log will appear as a row in your Sheet automatically.

1
Open Google Sheets and create a new spreadsheet. Name it anything you like.
2
Click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete all existing code. Paste the full contents of the apps-script.gs file provided alongside this HTML file. Click Save (Ctrl+S).
3
Click Deploy → New deployment. Type: Web app. Execute as: Me. Access: Anyone. Click Deploy and authorize when prompted.
4
Copy the Web App URL shown after deploying and paste it below.

🎯 Pace Reference Guide

Easy Pace
Conversational. +90–120 sec/km above 10K pace. ~65–70% max HR.
Tempo Pace
Comfortably hard. +15–25 sec/km above 10K pace. ~80–85% max HR.
Interval Pace
Goal 10K pace or slightly faster. ~88–93% max HR.
Long Run Pace
Same as Easy. Build time on feet, not speed.
Recovery Pace
Very easy. Almost uncomfortably slow. ~60% max HR.

📌 General Guidelines
10% rule: Never increase weekly mileage by more than 10% week-over-week. · Sleep: 7–9 hours — training adaptations happen during recovery. · Nutrition: Fuel hard days with carbohydrates; don't under-fuel. · Pain vs. soreness: Muscle soreness is expected; sharp or joint pain → rest and see a physio. · Google Sheets: Every "Log" button sends your data (status, pace, RPE, notes) directly to your Sheet as a new row.
